Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This policy explains how the mobile game Valet Parking ("the app") handles information. The app is developed by Hayri Anıl Çağlayan ("the developer", "we"). By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Information stored on your device

The app keeps your game progress, in-game coins and items, level results, settings (such as sound, music and vibration) and the optional player name you type for the weekly league on your device only. The player name is shown only on your own screen; it is never sent anywhere and other players cannot see it (league opponents are computer-generated). We do not create accounts, and the app does not ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

3. Information we collect

The app does not ask for your email or any account details, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

After you tap "Accept" on the first-launch card, the app sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies) so we can understand how the game is played and improve it. This includes:

  • Gameplay events: level started or finished, stars, moves, time, league promotions, in-game coin earnings and spending, taps on in-game cards and the billboard.
  • A random install identifier created by Unity for this installation of the app (not your Apple ID, not the advertising identifier).
  • Basic device information: device model, operating system version, app version and language.
  • Approximate country, which Unity derives from your internet connection. We do not collect precise location.

This data is used only for analytics. It is not linked to your identity, it is not used for advertising, and it is not sold. Your game progress, coins and items stay on your device; deleting the app removes them.

4. Third-party services

Analytics is provided by Unity Technologies; see the Unity Privacy Policy. The app currently contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. If such services are added, this page will be updated before they are used. The in-game billboard may link to other apps on the App Store; opening them takes you to the App Store and nothing is shared with those apps.

5. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

6. Your choices

You can reset your level progress from the in-game Settings screen, or remove all locally stored data by deleting the app. To ask for analytics data linked to your install identifier to be deleted, email us at the address below.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
