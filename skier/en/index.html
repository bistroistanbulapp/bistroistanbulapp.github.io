Skier — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Türkçe

In short: Skier collects no data and sends no data. The game has no internet connection. You are never asked to create an account. It contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

1. Data collected

None. The app never asks for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth or any other identifying information. It does not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data or calendars, and never requests these permissions.

The mountain, slopes and lifts in the game are drawn from map data shipped inside the app; your location is never used. No identifier, including the advertising identifier (IDFA), is read or created.

2. Stored on your device

So that you don't lose your progress, some information is stored only on your phone, in the app's own documents folder. It never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Game progress: Snow Coins, level, equipment, unlocked slopes, best scores and stars
  • Settings: sound, camera, riding style, look options
  • Performance log: frame rate and the phone's thermal state (to make the game run smoother; stays on the phone)
  • Your own notes: note flags you place in fly mode and an in-game screenshot of that moment (stays on the phone)

3. Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all of the above. As there are no records on any server, there is nothing for you to ask us to delete — we hold no data about you.

4. Internet connection

The app never connects to any server. All game content (mountain and terrain data, graphics, text) ships inside the app; sounds are generated on the device in real time. The game works fully in airplane mode.

5. Third parties

No ad network, analytics service, crash reporting tool, social media plug-in or any tracking software (SDK) is used. The game is made with the Unity game engine; the engine runs on the device and none of Unity's data-sending services are used in this app.

Because you get the app through the App Store or TestFlight, Apple's own privacy policy applies; Apple may collect download and crash statistics. Screenshots and comments you choose to send with TestFlight's "Send Beta Feedback" reach us through Apple. Details: apple.com/legal/privacy

6. Children

The game is for players of all ages. Since no data is collected, no data about children is collected either. There are no in-app purchases, ads, chat or interaction with other users.

7. Purchases

There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions or payments of any kind.

8. Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK give you the right to access, correct and request deletion of data processed about you. Skier processes no personal data, so in practice there is nothing for us to delete, transfer or correct.

9. Map data

Terrain: Copernicus EU-DEM (AWS Terrain Tiles). Slopes, lifts, buildings: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Forest cover: © ESA WorldCover 2021 (CC BY 4.0). This data contains no personal data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above is renewed. Any change that would require us to start collecting data will be stated clearly in the app update.

11. Contact

For questions and requests: info@bistroistanbulapp.com
