Skier — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Türkçe

In short: You never create an account in Skier, and we never ask for your email, phone number, real name or location. For leaderboards and cloud saves the game connects to Unity's game servers (Unity Gaming Services) with a random player ID, the player name you choose, your game progress and your scores. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. You can delete your account from inside the game at any time.

1. Data collected

When you open the game, Unity creates a random player ID for you (a "guest" account); you are not asked for any information. The following is stored on the server with this ID:

  • Player name: the name shown on leaderboards. If you don't choose one, you get a name like Kayakçı1234. Inappropriate names are blocked.
  • Cloud copy of your game progress: level, experience points, Snowcoin, unlocked runs, run statistics (best score, stars, time), owned and equipped items, learned tricks, the app version and date you first played, and the time of the last save.
  • Scores: your best score or time on each run, together with your level, app version and equipment power. They are visible to other players on the weekly and all-time leaderboards.
  • Run recording (ghost): the movement recording of your record runs and duel runs (position and pose on the slope, the tricks you do). It is used to check that the score is genuine and it is shown to other players: in duels, in races picked from the leaderboard and in challenges it appears as a rival ghost, with your name and your outfit.
  • Duels and challenges: your duel trophies, league and results; the challenges you send and receive (sender's player name, run, score, the chosen preset sentence, result) and the Snowcoins your ghost has won. When you challenge a friend, your name, the run and your score are written to their in-game challenge list. If you create a share link, anyone who opens it sees your name, the run and your score and can race your ghost; the link is valid for 30 days.
  • Game Center ID (only if Game Center is on on your iPhone): the player ID Apple issues for this game, used to link your progress to your Game Center account and bring it back when you change phones. Your Game Center nickname is only offered to you as a name suggestion; it is not put on the leaderboard unless you choose it.
  • Game Center friends list (only if you allow it; your iPhone asks separately): to show your friends' scores on the "Friends" tab of the leaderboard screen, or to challenge a friend, your friends' Game Center player IDs are sent to the server. They are not stored; they are only used at that moment to look up the scores and the friends who play the game.
  • Technical data (Unity): to run the service and prevent abuse, Unity's servers process the IP address of the connection and your device / operating system type, and may derive an approximate location (country / city level) from the IP address. Unity's in-game service libraries may also send usage information about the services (e.g. sign-in attempts) and anonymous crash data to monitor service health.

Not collected: email, phone number, real name, date of birth, precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising identifier (IDFA). The game never asks for these permissions. The mountain and runs are drawn from bundled map data; your phone's location is not used.

2. How the data is used

This data is used only to make the game work: leaderboards, backing up your progress in the cloud and restoring it when you change phones, and checking scores against cheating. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, selling, or matching with other apps, and we use no analytics tools. We do not track you across apps and websites.

3. What stays on your device

The following is stored only on your phone and never sent anywhere:

  • Settings: sound, language, steering sensitivity, camera, display options
  • Scores waiting to be sent (while offline) and the game's own temporary files
  • Performance log: frame rate and the phone's thermal state (on the phone only)
  • Your own notes: note flags you place in flight mode and their screenshots (on the phone only)

4. Deleting your data

In the game, go to Settings › Account › Delete my account to delete your account at any time (you are asked to confirm twice; an internet connection is needed). This deletes your player account at Unity, your cloud save, the scores linked to your account, your run recordings (ghosts), your duel and challenge records and your share links, and resets the progress on your phone. Deletion on the server may take a few days to complete. Deleting the app alone does not delete the data on the server. If you cannot open the game, write to the address below with your player ID (shown in Settings › Account) and we will delete it for you.

5. Internet connection

The game is fully playable offline. When online, it connects to Unity's servers at launch, at the end of a run and when the game goes to the background: sign-in, cloud save, sending scores and reading leaderboards. While offline, scores wait on the phone and are sent once you are connected.

6. Third parties

  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies): player sign-in (Authentication), cloud save (Cloud Save), leaderboards (Leaderboards) and score checks (Cloud Code). The data is processed on Unity's servers on our behalf; the servers may be outside your country. Details: Unity player privacy policy
  • Apple Game Center: if you use it, sign-in is handled by Apple and Apple's privacy policy applies.

No ad networks, analytics services or tracking software are used. Because you get the app through the App Store or TestFlight, Apple's own privacy policy also applies; download and crash statistics may be collected by Apple. Screenshots and comments you choose to send with "send feedback" in TestFlight reach us through Apple. Details: apple.com/legal/privacy

7. Children

The game is for players of all ages. No account is created, no contact details are requested, and there is no free chat or messaging (challenges only carry one of the game's four preset sentences); other players only see your player name, your score and your run recording (ghost). Inappropriate player names are filtered.

8. Purchases

There are currently no real-money in-app purchases or subscriptions. If they are added, payment will go through Apple and this page will be updated.

9. Retention

Data on the server is kept as long as your account exists. Weekly leaderboards reset every Monday; previous weeks' results are not kept.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR and Turkish KVKK you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data. You can change your player name and delete your account in Settings › Account; for any other request, write to the address below with your player ID.

11. Map data

Terrain: Copernicus EU-DEM (AWS Terrain Tiles). Runs, lifts, buildings: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Forest cover: © ESA WorldCover 2021 (CC BY 4.0). This data contains no personal data.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above is renewed. If we start collecting a new type of data, the app update will say so clearly.
